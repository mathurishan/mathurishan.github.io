Air New Zealand: Capacity, Reliability & Demand Intelligence in Power BI

A multi-page dashboard suite covering airline demand, operational reliability, route performance, and disruption-led capacity scenarios.

Problem

An airline network needs one view of demand, reliability and capacity: how traffic recovered after 2021, where cancellations and delays concentrate, and what a capacity shock such as engine groundings would mean.

Data

Public data on Air New Zealand arrivals, departures, cancellations, on-time performance and capacity, 2020 to 2025, brought into one Power BI data model with the model and its metric definitions written out in plain language.

What I built

A route, capacity and performance analysis in Power BI:

  • Executive summary: arrivals, departures, cancellation rate, on-time performance and capacity utilisation, with route-level cancellations
  • Passenger demand trends, 2020 to 2025
  • Operational performance: utilisation, cancellations and on-time performance by month
  • Demand and reliability trade-offs: traffic against cancellation rate and on-time arrival
  • Capacity shock scenario: the effect of engine groundings on capacity by segment

What changed

The dashboard shows passenger volumes recovering from the 2021 low through to 2025, cancellations peaking in June 2025, and which routes carry the highest cancellation rates month by month.

Tools

  • Power BI: data model and report
  • DAX: KPI measures
  • Power Query: data preparation
  • All Power BI work

Screenshots

Passenger Demand Trends (2020-2025) - This page focuses on long-run demand movement, using arrivals, departures, and YoY patterns to show how traffic changed through disruption and recovery.

Operational Performance Snapshot (2025) - A monthly operating view that compares utilisation, cancellations, and OTP, helping explain where short-term performance strengthened or came under pressure.

Demand-Reliability Trade-offs (2025) - Bubble-chart analysis is used to frame how traffic levels interact with cancellation rate and arrival OTP, making the demand-versus-service relationship easier to interpret.
